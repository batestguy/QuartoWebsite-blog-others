I am a statistician at Abubakar Tafawa Balewa University in Bauchi, Nigeria, where I am a Senior Statistician in the Academic Planning & Quality Assurance unit — building the institutional databases that sixty-odd departments report into, and advising the research projects that pass through them. Since 2017 I have also run JJMB Datascience Hub, an independent consultancy doing much the same work for clients in public health, finance and academia.

I am currently a PhD student in Statistics at ATBU, working on hybrid Bayesian deep learning for spatio-temporal risk forecasting. My MSc is also from ATBU and my BSc from the University of Maiduguri, finishing in at least the top two of my class in both instances. I put it that way on purpose. I am strongly against the GPA system: it compresses years of uneven and largely incomparable work into one number, and then invites everybody to treat that number as a finding rather than as the very lossy summary it is. Rank is the least misleading version I can give, and the actual evidence is the work — the posts here and my projects.

The methods I reach for most are Bayesian inference, survival analysis, spatial and geo-additive modelling, and time series forecasting — usually on survey or health data, where the answer worth reporting is an interval rather than a number. The fuller range is below.

This site is where I check that work instead of assuming it. A simulation that disagrees with what I expected is the most useful thing I can publish, so most of what is here is written to be run again rather than read once.

Selected work

  • Tobacco price intelligence — XGBoost demand forecast at 10.9% MAPE against a 13.8% naive baseline; fine-tuned FinBERT at 76.8% against a 71.0% majority class.
  • Crude blend optimizer — yield surrogates at R² ≥ 0.982 drive differential evolution to within $0.005/bbl of the exact LP optimum, with VaR/CVaR over 10,000 price scenarios.
  • Disability rights assistant — quotes the 2018 Act and the Constitution word for word with a citation on every excerpt; held-out strict recall 0.63–0.65, zero false refusals, WCAG 2.2 AA with no axe violations across 33 views.
  • Urban agriculture, where it concentrates — Moran’s I = 0.300 across 775 LGAs, only 19 significant after FDR control; density falls 66% per doubling of area, and the effect survives an explicit control for uneven OpenStreetMap coverage (ΔAIC ≈ 20).
  • curb-cut — arcsine Fay–Herriot small-area estimates of adult disability prevalence, with a bias-corrected back-transform and a parametric-bootstrap MSE.
